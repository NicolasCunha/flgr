## Decision

The decision section describes the architectural decision that was made, including the chosen solution. It should also include any trade-offs or compromises that were made in making the decision. Technical details, diagrams, and other supporting information can be included to provide a clear understanding of the decision.

## Alternatives Considered

## Consequences

The consequences section outlines the implications of the architectural decision, including any potential risks, benefits, and impacts on the system's architecture. It should also include any follow-up actions or considerations that may be necessary as a result of the decision.

If no consequences are expected, this section should be simply detailed as "N/A".

## References

References to any relevant documentation, research, or other sources that were used in making the architectural decision. This section should include links to any relevant documents, articles, or other resources that provide additional context or information about the decision.
